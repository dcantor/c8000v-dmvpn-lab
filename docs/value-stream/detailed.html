Method

Measured times come from the job records the portal keeps (webapp/runs/): every log line carries its time, so a task is the span between the lines that start and end it. The test suites are timed per test by Robot Framework.

Derived times split a measured total using the code. The VyOS day-0 push waits for its prompt in 2-second read windows (tools/vyos_console.py), so its 165 configuration lines take at least 330 s; the kernel log shows the router itself booted in about 32 s. The C8000v bootstrap (lab.sh) boots, pushes day-0, reloads for the licence level after a fixed 60-second sleep, boots again and polls RESTCONF every 10 s; its phases are split by those rules and its console timings, and only the 561 s total is measured.

Estimates are the operator's planning time and the future times that depend on work not yet done (a scoped Nautobot seed, customer-scoped tests, a golden image). Future times of tasks moved into parallel with the boot count as zero on the critical path.

The C8000v job (cust5, Sep 27) ran before the configuration snapshots existed; its snapshot tasks use cust7's 4 s. The test suite has grown since (74 tests for cust7, fewer for cust5), which is part of why cust7's tests took longer.
